# Pure Derma Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Pure Derma Dermatology Clinic LLC is a Dubai-based dermatology and medical aesthetics clinic located at Dubai Hills Mall. The clinic offers medical and cosmetic dermatology, laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, facials and non-surgical aesthetic treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic's specialist services, making treatment options easier to discover and creating a more direct consultation enquiry journey for prospective patients.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging

- Replaces the generic introductory messaging with a more distinctive dermatology and skincare-focused headline.
- Introduces the clinic's main services, including dermatology, facial treatments and laser procedures.
- Provides clearer consultation and treatment exploration calls to action.

**Issue addressed:** The original headline, "Healthy skin begins here," did not sufficiently communicate the clinic's specialist offering or give visitors a clear reason to explore its services.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery

- Presents the main treatment categories more prominently.
- Highlights individual services such as HydraFacial, Dermapen, mesotherapy, skin boosters and laser hair removal.
- Creates clearer pathways from treatment categories to their relevant service pages.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage required visitors to explore further to understand the available treatments, making service discovery less straightforward.

### 3. More Accessible Appointment and Enquiry Journey

- Improves the visibility of telephone and WhatsApp contact options.
- Makes consultation calls to action more prominent throughout the homepage.
- Prioritises a shorter enquiry process and accessible mobile contact options.

**Issue addressed:** The original website did not make the journey from treatment discovery to contacting the clinic sufficiently direct and consistent.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Establishes a cohesive, professional aesthetic that reflects the clinic's dermatology and medical beauty positioning.
- **Layout and Hierarchy:** Gives greater prominence to the main headline, treatment categories and consultation actions.
- **User Experience:** Reduces unnecessary exploration by making key services easier to identify.
- **Navigation:** Creates clearer connections between treatment categories and individual service pages.
- **Mobile Accessibility:** Prioritises visible contact options and easier access to consultation actions on mobile devices.
- **Overall Appearance:** Balances the clinic's aesthetic identity with clear medical service communication and a more conversion-focused homepage structure.

---

**Project Focus:** Stronger first impressions, easier treatment discovery and a simpler consultation journey.
