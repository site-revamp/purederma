Some quick answers to help you decide what to ask the clinic. For treatment-specific advice, contact Pure Derma directly.

Where is Pure Derma Clinic located?

Pure Derma Clinic is located on the first floor of Dubai Hills Mall, near Storm Coaster Parking, Dubai, UAE.

What types of treatments does the clinic offer?

The clinic publishes dermatology, facial and laser treatment categories. Examples include Hydrafacial, Dermapen, mesotherapy, chemical peels, skin boosters, dermal fillers, microneedling, laser hair removal and tattoo removal.

How can I ask whether a treatment is suitable for me?

Call the clinic or send a WhatsApp message to discuss your concern and ask about arranging a consultation. Treatment suitability should be assessed by an appropriate clinician.

What are the clinic's opening hours?

Published hours are Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 am to 10:00 pm, and Friday and Saturday, 10:00 am to 12:00 am. Contact the clinic to confirm before travelling.
